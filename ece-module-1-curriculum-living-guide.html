Build understanding

Curriculum is more than a document. It has four layers. They are related, but never perfectly identical.

Intended (Yang dimaksudkan)What frameworks and goals say we want children to learn
Planned (Yang direncanakan)What educators deliberately design
Enacted (Yang dilaksanakan)What actually happens through people, materials and relationships
Experienced (Yang dialami anak)What the child encounters, feels, understands and learns

Curriculum is a compass, not a script. A compass gives direction without prescribing every step.

A map

Routes are visible but the journey may feel predetermined.

A script

A compass

Clear direction + professional judgement.

A holistic routeJalur yang holistik

Build understanding

Indonesia’s 2025 Guide to Learning and Assessment (Panduan Pembelajaran dan Asesmen) describes deep learning pembelajaran mendalam. It aims at eight graduate profile dimensions dimensi profil lulusan and rests on three principles, carried out through thought, heart, feeling and body olah pikir, olah hati, olah rasa, olah raga.

Mindful berkesadaran

Learners know what they are learning and why, feel comfortable, make choices, and are curious.

Meaningful bermakna

Learning connects to real life and context. Children can use what they know. Families and community are sources of knowledge.

Joyful menggembirakan

A positive, challenging atmosphere with interest, curiosity, room for initiative and creativity, and the “Aha!” moment.

Learning also moves through three experiences: understanding memahami, applying mengaplikasi and reflecting merefleksi.

Have a go

What could this look like for a four-year-old? Write what you might see or hear, so the principle becomes observable.

Compare with some ideas

Mindful: curious, aware, making choices, trying strategies. Meaningful: connected to life, relevant to context, useful beyond the task. Joyful: positive and challenging, motivating, creative, “Aha!” moments. And joyful is not the same as simply entertained.

A window on another curriculumJendela ke kurikulum lain

Build understanding

Australia’s Early Years Learning Framework (EYLF V2.0, 2022) is built from a vision, principles, practices and learning outcomes. We use it as a comparative lens, not a replacement for your curriculum.

Some of its practices: responsiveness to children, play-based learning with intentionality, holistic and integrated approaches, learning environments, cultural responsiveness, and assessment and evaluation for learning. Each depends on professional judgement.

Have a go

If a curriculum already told educators exactly what to do, why would responsiveness be necessary?

6about 20 min

Audit my settingMeninjau tempat saya mengajar

Build understanding

The programme’s Terms of Reference report findings from the 2024 Learning Environment Survey (Sulingjar): only 11.44% of institutions show a high level of effective learning planning, 19.24% consistently use pedagogy suited to early childhood, and 23.63% effectively build children’s foundational competencies.

One reason it gives: the curriculum is treated as an administrative document more than a practical guide.

How true is each statement in your setting? This is a mirror for you, not a score.
