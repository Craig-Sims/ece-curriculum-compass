What you will be able to doApa yang akan dapat Anda lakukan

  1. Explain the principles of authentic assessment in early childhood education/PAUD: formative assessment first; observation as a primary data source; assessment that is fair, objective and educational; assessment as part of the teaching and learning cycle (plan, teach, assess, reflect).
  2. Observe and document children’s learning using techniques suited to early childhood: anecdotal notes, observational checklists, work samples, photo documentation and portfolios, separating description from interpretation.
  3. Interpret documented evidence holistically against the three Capaian Pembelajaran (CP) elements, identifying strengths and next teaching steps. (Religious Values and Character; Identity (Jati Diri); and Foundations of Literacy, Mathematics, Science, Technology, Engineering, and Art.)
  4. Use assessment information to plan responsive learning experiences and communicate children’s progress to families in clear, strengths-based language.

Build understanding

What the Guide says (Panduan Pembelajaran dan Asesmen, 2025):

Learning and assessment are one cycle

Assessment gives information about what learning to design next, and learning gives new evidence to assess.

Observation and performance

Assessment is not only written or oral tests. It can use observation (children observed regularly over time) and performance techniques (practice, product, project and portfolio).

In PAUD, the whole child

What is monitored is not only progress in the CP elements, but the child’s growth and development as a whole.

Fair berkeadilan

Not affected by background or identity. Accommodations for children with special needs. Teachers set criteria and share them. For the move from PAUD to primary school, reading, writing and counting tests are not used as entry criteria.

Objective objektif

Based on factual information about development or learning, with clear, agreed criteria and not personal impressions. Uses a range of techniques, including observation.

Educational edukatif

Results give feedback to educators, children and parents so learning improves. Assessment strengthens the learning process and does not only judge results.
