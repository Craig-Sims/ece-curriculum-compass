Goal settingMenetapkan tujuan

Clear goals can support change. It provides direction, focus and times for celebration when they are achieved.

Watch this video: SMART goals

Let’s have a go with setting goals with an areas that you are most familiar with – your day-to-day life. Have a go at setting a life SMART goal. Follow these steps

Have a go
  • Make a full list of things you would like to achieve in your life
  • Prioritise (relevance for this time in your life)
  • Select the most important for your life at present (timely, start small with just one)
  • Write major steps to achieving this one goal (actionable)
  • How long will these steps take (time)
  • What does success look like? (measurable)
  • Tie it all together into a specific statement (specific)

RESULT: Goal that is SMART 😊
